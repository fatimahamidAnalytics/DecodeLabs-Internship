## Project 2: Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

**Dataset:** E-Commerce Orders (1000 rows, 14 columns)

**Tools Used:** Microsoft Excel (PivotTables, Descriptive Statistics)

**Key Findings:**
- Total Revenue: PKR 1,051,995 across 1,000 orders
- Top Product: Chair & Printer (almost equal revenue ~PKR 195,000 each)
- ~50% orders were Cancelled or Returned (major business risk)
- Instagram is the top customer acquisition channel
- Credit Card is the most preferred payment method
- 5 high-value outlier orders detected via IQR method
